FAQ

Here's the answer to the most common questions

How does white labeling work with Digieno Partners?

We build and manage the digital products websites, apps, SaaS, cloud, and AI solutions while you add your branding and resell them to your clients. No one knows we're behind the scenes. You take full credit for the work.

Can I get custom features for my clients?

Absolutely. We work closely with you to understand your client's unique requirements. Whether it's custom integrations, specific functionality, or unique design elements, we'll build exactly what your agency needs to close the deal.

How does licensing and pricing work?

We offer flexible partnership plans that scale with your agency. You pay us a flat fee or revenue share, and you set your own pricing with your clients. There are no hidden fees or long-term commitments. Just transparent, fair pricing.

How do I extend the default setup?

Our solutions are built with scalability in mind. As your agency grows, you can easily add new features, integrate third-party tools, and expand functionality. We're here to support you every step of the way with dedicated developer resources.

How can I request new features or services?

Our partnership model is built on collaboration. You have direct access to our product team, and we prioritize feature requests based on partner demand. If enough agencies need it, we build it and you benefit from the shared development costs.
