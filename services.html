FAQ

Here's the answer to the most common questions

How does white labeling work with Digieno Partners?

We build and manage the digital products — websites, apps, SaaS, cloud, and AI solutions — while you add your branding and resell them to your clients. No one knows we're behind the scenes. You take full credit for the work and keep your client relationships.

Can I get custom features for my clients?

Absolutely. We work closely with you to understand your client's unique requirements. Whether it's custom integrations, specific functionality, or unique design elements, we'll build exactly what your agency needs to close the deal and impress your clients.

How does pricing and payment work?

We offer flexible partnership plans that scale with your agency. You can choose between project-based pricing or monthly subscriptions depending on the service. You set your own pricing with your clients. There are no hidden fees or long-term commitments.

How do I get started with a service?

Simply click "Get this service" on any card or contact us directly. We'll schedule a free consultation to understand your needs, provide a detailed proposal, and timeline. Once approved, our team gets to work building your white label solution.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes! All our white label services include ongoing support and maintenance. You get dedicated account managers, priority support channels, and regular updates to keep your products running smoothly. We're here for the long haul.
